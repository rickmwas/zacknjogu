# Project Overview & Domain Research: Zack Njogu MCA Campaign Website

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Mission
This document outlines the strategic research, user persona analysis, and digital campaign framework for **Zack Njogu**, candidate for **Member of County Assembly (MCA)** representing **Maai Mahiu Ward** (Naivasha Constituency, Nakuru County, Kenya) under the **Jubilee Party**.

The campaign's primary digital objective is to establish an unassailable digital presence that reflects **modern, accountable, transparent, and technology-driven leadership**. In Kenyan local elections, voters frequently demand tangible proof of community commitment, accessibility, and actionable solutions to local economic and infrastructural challenges. The website serves as the single source of truth for Zack Njogu's vision, manifesto, community track record, and volunteer engagement.

---

## 2. Deep Local Context: Maai Mahiu Ward Profile
Maai Mahiu is a critical economic and transport node within Nakuru County and Naivasha Sub-County. Understanding the local electorate is essential for tailored web messaging and UX:

- **Geographic & Economic Drivers**:
  - Transport & Logistics hub (Maai Mahiu Inland Container Depot / Standard Gauge Railway / major highway transit node).
  - High concentration of youth engaged in logistics, small-scale trade (MSMEs), transport (Boda Boda / Matatu operators), sand harvesting, and agriculture.
  - Water infrastructure, road networks, unemployment, and market sanitation are prominent community concerns.
- **Voter Demographics & Digital Behavior**:
  - **Youth (18–35)**: Heavily active on mobile platforms (WhatsApp, TikTok, Facebook, X). High sensitivity to data costs; demand ultra-fast, lightweight web pages.
  - **Business Owners & Farmers**: Seek clear policy promises on local trade enablement, revenue transparency, agricultural support, and infrastructure improvement.
  - **Women Groups & Community Leaders**: Value community empowerment initiatives, bursary transparency, and direct communication channels.
  - **Network Conditions**: Internet connectivity across Maai Mahiu spans 3G/4G with occasional bandwidth drops. Over **92%** of web sessions will originate from mobile smartphones (Android dominant).

## 3. Political Digital Strategy: Balance of Party & Candidate
- **Candidate as the Core Product**: Zack Njogu is presented as a visionary, competent, and accessible leader. The branding emphasizes personal integrity, actionable manifesto pillars, and tech-enabled governance.
- **Jubilee Party Integration**: The Jubilee brand identity (Red, Yellow, Green, Dove symbol) is integrated respectfully as a supporting institutional foundation, preventing political noise from crowding out candidate messaging.
- **Anti-Gimmick Technology Vision**: Technology is framed as an operational tool for county governance—improving bursary application efficiency, public participation tracking, and ward issue reporting.

## 4. User Personas & Journey Mapping

### Persona 1: Brian (24, Transport/Logistics Worker, Youth Leader)
- **Goals**: Wants to know Zack's plan for youth empowerment, vocational training, and bursary allocation.
- **Pain Points**: Frustrated by empty political promises; low data bundles.
- **Web Journey**: Accesses site via WhatsApp link on low-end smartphone → Hero section loads instantly (<1.2s) → Skips to Youth & Economy Manifesto section → Signs up as a Youth Volunteer via 1-click form.

### Persona 2: Mama Wanjiku (42, Small Business Owner & Chama Leader)
- **Goals**: Evaluates Zack's track record on market infrastructure, water access, and trader support.
- **Pain Points**: Needs clear, trustworthy information; prefers simple navigation and readable text.
- **Web Journey**: Clicks Facebook link → Reads About Zack bio → Explores Community Initiatives → Accesses WhatsApp Campaign Channel directly.

### Persona 3: Elder Mwangi (58, Resident & Landowner)
- **Goals**: Assesses Zack's leadership credibility, party alignment, and ward development roadmap.
- **Pain Points**: Skeptical of flashiness or AI-generated political hype.
- **Web Journey**: Navigates desktop/tablet → Reads full Manifesto → Views Gallery of real community engagements → Uses Contact Form for direct inquiry.

## 5. Anti-AI Design Philosophy & Editorial Aesthetic Rules
To ensure the website looks and feels like a bespoke design by a premier product design studio (inspired by Apple, Stripe, Linear, Notion, Vercel, and Airbnb), the following explicit guardrails are enforced:

### Eliminating AI Design Clichés:
1. **NO Over-saturated Neon Gradients or Glowing Borders**: Color usage must be grounded, calm, and purposeful.
2. **NO Generic Stock Illustrations or AI Avatars**: Only real candidate photography, real community engagement photos, and custom geometric iconography.
3. **NO Repetitive Generic Card Grids**: Layouts must feature varied editorial rhythm—staggered feature splits, clean typography focus, structural whitespace, and rich content hierarchy.
4. **NO Hero Section Clichés**: No floating 3D spheres, oversized floating tech glass, or generic particle animations.
5. **NO Marketing Hype Text**: Copy must be clean, crisp, grounded in local facts, and authoritative.

### Editorial & Modern Product Aesthetic Principles:
- **Typography-Driven Hierarchy**: Inter / Outfit paired with crisp sans-serif body text; strict typographic scale with comfortable line height (`1.6` for copy).
- **Subtle Micro-Interactions**: Smooth CSS transitions (`duration-200 ease-out`), subtle button press feedback, and layout shifts kept to zero (`CLS < 0.01`).
- **Tactile Whitespace & Calibrated Layout**: Clean separation of content sections using subtle 1px border dividers (`border-neutral-200` dark mode `border-neutral-800`).
